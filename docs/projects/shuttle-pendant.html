Shuttle Pendant

  • 3d printing

I challenged myself to 3d print at the very limits of what is possible. It took many tries with many settings adjustment, but I finally got a clean print with all of the tiny orbital manoeuvring thrusters correctly formed. It was based on NASA's own 3d model of the shuttle.

Painted using small drops of paint on the tip of needles under about 30x magnification.

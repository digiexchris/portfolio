TW200

The most tractorly of bikes gets a more tractorly of a wheel

  • Motorcycles

A friend and I both own this little dual sport. Nicknamed the goat by the community because it can climb any hill, and it's extremely easy to ride.


We ride in sand a lot, and the stock tires are not the best at that. Since this is a motorcycle spec rim, ATV tires don't fit perfectly. Motorcycle rims are slightly larger diameter than automotive and ATV rims. Many people put ATV tires on anyway, and use dangerous pressures to seat the bead. I decided to machine a pair of hubs for us in order to mount common light weight trailer rims that fit ATV tires well. The bearing preload I calculated is so good that the bearings in these hubs have long outlived the stock bearings in the stock hubs. It really does climb anything now.

There are a few other modifications I've made to the bike:

  • lengthened the swingarm 2" to provide better mud and rock clearance with the larger tire
  • moved the suspension pivot to raise the bike 1.5" for my height
  • extended the forks 1.5" to keep the steering angles correct
  • welded up a very light weight but strong aluminum luggage rack
  • machined a longer carb pilot mixture needle so I can adjust it just by reaching down under the carb. This lets me get the perfect mixture for the temperature of the day.

PS4 HDMI Connector Replacement

Tight spaces

  • Electronics
  • Repair

This was a tricky one. The customer noticed damage to the pins inside the connector. They brought me the PS4 and a new connector, which matched up perfectly.

The hard part was the tight quarters. There are quite tall components on 3 sides of the connector close to the connector so it's hard to get an iron in there and hard to keep hot air off of the more sensitive stuff near by. Careful use of kapton tape saved the day, as well as the trick of wiping the tip of the iron across the pins to pick up excess solder bridging pins. I ended up making a special soldering iron tip on my lathe to reach in there.

The pins don't line up perfectly, but the locating dowels on the connector shell constrains it in position on the board, and the original was slightly offset like that too. It tested without shorts

CR450 To CR650 Stem

  • Suspension
  • Motorcycles

A member of the ADVRider Manitoba motorcycle group approached me for help with a suspension conversion for his CR650 dual sport motorcycle. He needed a new steering stem machined to adapt the triple trees to his bike's bearings.

I suggested 7075 for strength and pull-out resistance on the threads, and he ordered some. I asked that he provide the CR450 and CR650 steering stems, and both the old and new triple trees, so I could match dimensions without knowing the originally engineered tolerance band for either.

The fit in the lower triple tree is a shrink fit, and requires about 200C to make it so you can drop the stem into it with just light finger pressure.

What a high current amplifier MOSFET looks like inside

  • Electronics
  • Repair
  • Amateur Radio

I was repairing a 100w radio amplifier that fused one of the two mosfets, as indicated by the oscilloscope only reading half of the wave. It failed so bad it popped the epoxy cap off and let us see inside. You can see the Emitter (top black row) Base (Pinkish row at the bottom) and the collector (middle black row). Look at all of those microscopic gold wires!

You're seeing it at 10x and 30x magnification.

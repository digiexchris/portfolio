ESP-Prog power rail repair

Oops

  • Electronics
  • Repair

This one was my fault. I was using an ESP-Prog JTAG board to work on an STM32F103, and crossed my 3v3 and gnd lines. Luckily, the ESP-Prog sacrifices itself by blowing the reverse protection diode. It would have been nice if they used the mosfet trick (won't even conduct in reverse) or at least put a fuse in there.

I used a FLIR to spot that the diode was unusually hot, then did a multimeter diode test. It was passing current in both directions, which browned out the power rail thankfully, before any reverse voltage could get to the MCU.

I didn't have a suitable SMD diode on hand, so I used a similarly rated through hole diode.

Icom 706 MKIIG Intermediate Frequency Tap

  • Electronics
  • Modification
  • Amateur Radio

On a non-SDR radio, you can sometimes tap where the intermediate frequency of the receiver is output. This allows you to get a relatively wide bandwidth view, within the bandwidth of the IF filter anyway. If I remember correctly, on the 706MKIIG it gives you nearly 1/4 of the 20M band in view at once. You can then pass it to an SDR to create a panadapter, or a visual view of the activity on the band. You can also use the output to use the SDR as a final tuner. Some SDRs, like the Airspy HF, have very good decoders, and can sometimes outperform the final stage of the existing receiver.

The IF is available off of one side of the resistor valued 331 just to the front of the adjustable inductor, on the front-left of the radio with the radio facing you.
